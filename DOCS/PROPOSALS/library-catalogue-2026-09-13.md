# SanctissiMissa preliminary Library and Bookstore catalogue

Research snapshot: 2026-09-13. **Proposed catalogue, not a list of products cleared for sale.** This document supplies 61 book/work-set records and eight proposed prayer collections, grouped by category and attributed by author. Multi-part works are labelled explicitly. No text corpus was imported, no commercial rights were granted, and no products or prices were published by this research.

## Product and rights model

The Library holds acquired, included, and personally imported works. The Bookstore offers optional, carefully prepared editions: verified text, useful navigation, offline download, paragraph-level references, and integration with the reader. A purchase must identify the exact edition and what the buyer receives. Public-domain status belongs to a particular text/edition in a territory; it does not make every modern translation, introduction, cover, scan package, performance, or recording reusable.

Provide the same bookmarks, highlights, text annotations, personal recordings, reading position, and export behaviour for included and purchased readable works. Personal annotations and recordings belong to the user and should survive expiry of an optional service subscription. Distinguish an outright edition purchase from a subscription to services. The payment architecture belongs in the architecture/checklist; this catalogue's identifiers are provisional editorial identifiers, not processor product IDs.

Recommended commercial positioning: charge for the prepared SanctissiMissa edition and useful services, disclose the public-domain underlying work and source, and keep a useful free starting collection. Do not imply ownership of the original text or charge again to open a user's own imported copy.

### Territory and source rules verified for this proposal

- The US Copyright Office currently states that works published in the United States before January 1, 1931 are in the public domain. This is a 2026 rule of thumb for the stated class, not a universal rule for every foreign, unpublished, revised, or audio work. [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/)
- Canada's general term changed to life plus 70 years on December 30, 2022; already-public-domain works did not regain protection. Assess every translator, editor, and other contributor and applicable special rules. A US catalogue label alone does not establish Canadian status. [CIPO](https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en/copyright?wbdisable=true), [Copyright Board of Canada](https://cb-cda.gc.ca/en/faq)
- Project Gutenberg's catalogue and embedded licences must both be checked. Its trademark licence affects commercial redistribution of Gutenberg-branded files. The documented unrestricted-text route requires removing the Gutenberg branding/licence association as specified by its terms; retain source provenance in the editorial record. Do not blindly wrap and sell a Gutenberg EPUB. [Project Gutenberg licence](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license.html)
- CCEL says its editions can contain protected introductions, covers, and other material, and requests permission for republication/commercial use. CCEL links below are **research and comparison leads**; use a separately cleared historical source or obtain permission before copying its digital editions into paid products. [CCEL copyright policy](https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html)
- For example, the linked *Story of a Soul* names Thomas N. Taylor (1873–1963) as translator. This illustrates why neither Thérèse's death date nor a US public-domain label settles every territory. Canadian pre-extension status must be assessed separately; life-plus-70 territories may differ. [Gutenberg edition record](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16772)

## Category and author navigation

Store navigation should combine category, author, language, edition/translation, format, availability, owned/downloaded state, and liturgical tradition. Use author aliases for search while preserving the printed attribution: “Alphonsus Liguori” / “Alfonso Maria de' Liguori”; “Teresa of Ávila” / “Teresa of Jesus”; “Thomas à Kempis”; “Thomas Aquinas”; “Guéranger” / “Gueranger”. A multi-author compilation must appear under its actual contributors without presenting the editor as author of ancient texts.

Author pages can immediately group Aquinas (C003, C024–C028), Augustine (C004–C005), Newman (C029–C030, C048), Teresa (C034–C036), John of the Cross (C037–C039), Francis de Sales (C032–C033), Hammer (C053–C054), and Guéranger (C060–C061). C003 needs attribution that describes Newman's editorial/translation involvement precisely after volume-level review.

## Minimum record before enabling purchase/download

The implementation contract should require an edition manifest containing:

1. Stable work and edition identifiers, title, author/contributor roles, language, exact printing/translation, edition date and publisher, source URL and retrieval date.
2. Original-file hash, permitted source assets, territories assessed, rights rationale and evidence, review state, and exclusions for protected additions. Unknown fields keep publication disabled; they must not be populated with guessed names or dates.
3. Complete contents, source page/section mapping, proofread text version, download size/format/hash, accessibility status, and explicit scope/omissions. A landing-page download link is not proof of complete contents.
4. Stable paragraph/verse anchors and a migration map when text changes, so highlights, notes and personal recordings still resolve after a corrected edition is downloaded.
5. Bundle membership and exact purchased edition; explicit distinction between permanent purchase and service subscription. No live processor identifier is inferred from C001–C061.

Text, notation, cover images, audio performances, translations and newly written notes have separate provenance. This matters especially for the chant area: an ancient chant melody does not automatically clear a recent score engraving or a choir recording. Gregorian chant content and parish planning are specified by the companion architecture proposal, not by this book catalogue.

## Research completion and limits

The 61 rows are an intentionally preliminary editorial acquisition list. Evidence is strongest for directly identified Gutenberg items and dated institutional scans. Several classic titles remain at source-lead stage because identifying an attractive book is easier than proving the exact digital edition is complete and reusable. No row is `READY`; no row authorizes a live purchase. This separation is the intended input to the GLM checklist so it can seed a useful preview catalogue without inventing rights metadata or shipping unreviewed books.
