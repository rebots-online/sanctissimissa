## Decisions (defaults, recorded in docs)

- **Paywalls (graphical, dashboard Paywall Editor)**: "Companion Unlock" attached to `companion_unlock`; "Bookstore" attached to `bookstore` (copy states cash or $ROCHE credits). Honest copy per LS.06: real prices/terms, no fake discounts, no fake ownership.
