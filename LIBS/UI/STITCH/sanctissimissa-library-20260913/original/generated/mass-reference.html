Mass of the Catechumens

Prayers at the Foot of the Altar

Introibo ad altare Dei • Ad Deum qui laetificat juventutem meam
📖 Open this part in Missal Reader 🎵 Learn its Chant 📚 Related Books (Catena, Cochem)
Prescribed Rubric (Ritus Servandus in Celebratione Missae, III.4):
The Priest, standing at the foot of the altar, makes a profound inclination, signs himself with the sign of the cross from forehead to breast, saying with an audible voice: In nómine Patris... The server kneels at the left side behind the celebrant.

Ceremonial Sequence:

The priest and ministers approach the altar. In the 1962 Missal, Psalm 42 (Judica me, Deus) is recited antiphonally with the ministers or sacred servers. This expresses the priest's preparation and humility before mounting the steps to the Holy of Holies.

Following Psalm 42, the Confiteor is recited first by the celebrant bowed profoundly, then by the ministers, acknowledging personal sinfulness before Almighty God and the heavenly court.

Celebrant Standing at bottom step; profound bow during Confiteor; ascends altar steps at Oremus / Aufer a nobis.
Sacred Ministers / Servers Kneeling on the lowest step or pavement; bowing toward the celebrant at et tibi, pater.
Faithful / Congregation Kneeling throughout the Prayers at the Foot of the Altar (Low Mass and Missa Cantata practice).
