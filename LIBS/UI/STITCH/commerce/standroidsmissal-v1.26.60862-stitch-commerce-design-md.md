# St. Android's Missal — Design System

## Brand rationale
**Parchment & ink, illuminated by the liturgical color of the day.** The Traditional Latin Mass and Divine Office rendered as a navigable subway map. The visual language is a hand-illuminated missal: warm parchment, deep ink, gilt gold, and liturgical maroon — reverent, scholarly, trustworthy. Commerce surfaces (plans, bookstore, account) must feel like the same missal, not a bolted-on SaaS billing page.

## Palette
- **Primary**: liturgical maroon `#7a1f2b` (line-faithful) — the faithful's line; used for primary actions and plan emphasis.
- **Secondary / accent**: illuminated gold `#d4a017` (bright) / `#a97e13` (deep) — gilt; reserved for the recommended plan, badges, and highlights.
- **Background**: parchment `#f6f0e2`, vellum `#fbf7ee`, parchment-deep `#ece2cc` for borders.
- **Ink**: `#221c14` (body), `#4a4034` (soft), `#8a7d6a` (faint).
- **Office blue** `#274b69` used sparingly for informational/secondary links.
- **Rubric red** `#9c2733` for rubrics and important notices only.
- **Liturgical accent** (seasonal): green `#3f7a52`, purple `#5d3a80`, rose `#b76a7d` — orthogonal data-color, never used for commerce chrome.

## Typography
- **Headlines**: serif (Iowan Old Style / Palatino) — the voice of the missal; used for screen titles, plan names, bookstore titles.
- **Body**: serif for reading; sans-serif (system-ui / Segoe UI) for UI chrome, buttons, prices, and metadata.
- **Labels/meta**: sans, small caps, letter-spaced.

## Shape & motion
- Roundness 8–10dp on cards; soft, hand-bound feel. No harsh corners, no glassmorphism on commerce surfaces.
- Motion restrained and meaningful only.

## Modes
Canonical commerce system is LIGHT (parchment reading surface). Dark mode exists in the app but commerce screens are designed light-first.
