Corpus Provenance

The corpus is László Kiss' Divinum Officium flat-text tree, re-realized as a graph and vector SQLite database. Latin is normative; the English is a modular translation and may be absent for some sections.

Divinum Officium — vendored snapshot, see PROVENANCE.md ▸ Vulgata Clementina — Latin scripture gap-fill ▸ Douay-Rheims — English scripture gap-fill ▸
