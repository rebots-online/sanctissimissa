On the Unjust Steward

The parable is uncomfortable because our Lord praises a swindler. He does not praise the fraud — He praises the foresight. The steward, told he is finished, acts decisively while he still has standing to act.

"Homo quidam erat dives, qui habébat víllicum."

— Luc 16:1

We are all stewards on notice. The question the Gospel puts is not whether the accounting comes, but what we do with the interval before it
