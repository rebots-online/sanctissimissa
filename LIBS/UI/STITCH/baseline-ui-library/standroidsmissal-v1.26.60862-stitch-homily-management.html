Semantic links for "Fraternal Correction" (Matt 18:15-20)

In today's Gospel, Jesus offers us a profoundly challenging, yet deeply practical, teaching on how to handle conflict within the community. "If your brother sins against you, go and tell him his fault between you and him alone."

Notice the order. He does not say, "post about it online," or "gossip about it with your friends." He demands the hardest thing first: direct, private confrontation motivated by love.

Note: Connect this to Ezekiel 33 (First Reading) - the role of the watchman. We are responsible for one another.
